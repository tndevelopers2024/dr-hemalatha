Your first visit

Getting started is simple

  1. Step 1

    Book your visit

    Call, WhatsApp or use the booking form. Pick a time that suits you.

  2. Step 2

    Meet Dr. Hemalatha

    Bring your previous reports, scans and list of medicines. Your family is welcome.

  3. Step 3

    Get your personal plan

    Leave with clear next steps: tests, scans, follow-ups and what to watch for.

Your journey with us

Every step, with the same caring hands

  1. Planning

    Pre-pregnancy check, folic acid, health review.

  2. Weeks 1–12

    Confirming pregnancy, first scans, managing nausea.

  3. Weeks 13–27

    Anomaly scan, baby's growth, diet and exercise.

  4. Weeks 28–40

    Birth plan, labour signs, pain-relief choices.

  5. Delivery

    Dr. Hemalatha attends your birth personally.

  6. After birth

    Your recovery, breastfeeding and baby's first weeks.
