Laparoscopy — smaller cuts, less pain, quicker recovery

Dr. Hemalatha Your doctor at every visit Book a consultation →

What is it?

Laparoscopy is surgery done through 2–4 tiny cuts (about 0.5–1 cm) in the tummy, instead of one large cut. A thin camera shows the inside of the body on a screen, and the surgeon uses fine instruments to treat the problem.

Why women prefer it

Open surgeryLaparoscopy
One large cut2–4 tiny cuts
More pain after surgeryLess pain
Longer hospital stayOften home in 1–2 days
Weeks to recoverBack to daily routine sooner
Bigger scarVery small scars
Higher risk of incisional herniaLess incidence of hernia
